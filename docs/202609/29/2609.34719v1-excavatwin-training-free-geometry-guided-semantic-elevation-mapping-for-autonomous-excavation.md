## 摘要
自主挖掘需要一种能够联合捕捉地形几何与任务相关语义的空间表示。现有挖掘建图大多以高程为中心，而通用语义模型在非结构化室外场景中仍不稳定。我们提出 ExcavaTwin，一个纯视觉、几何引导的语义高程建图框架，无需针对挖掘任务进行专门训练。给定多视角 RGB 图像，该框架：1）利用冻结的视觉模型重建场景几何与语义观测；2）推导地形与非地形的几何支撑；3）执行几何约束的多视角语义融合，以抑制不合理预测并恢复不完整观测；4）将融合后的状态投影为面向任务的语义高程图。在公开数据集和真实挖掘场景上的实验证明了可靠的几何与语义感知。在真实挖掘中，系统在动态修改区域实现了约 1.4 s 的平均更新间隔和 12.74 cm 的平均高程误差。较大误差主要出现在地形快速变化以及机器运动引起的瞬态视觉干扰期间。

## Abstract
Autonomous excavation requires a spatial representation that jointly captures terrain geometry and task-relevant semantics. Existing excavation mapping is largely elevation-centric, while generic semantic models remain unstable in unstructured outdoor scenes. We present ExcavaTwin, a pure-vision geometry-guided semantic elevation mapping framework without excavation-specific training. Given multi-view RGB images, the framework: 1) reconstructs scene geometry and semantic observations using frozen vision models; 2) derives terrain and non-terrain geometric support; 3) performs geometry-constrained multi-view semantic fusion to suppress implausible predictions and recover incomplete observations; and 4) projects the fused state into a task-oriented semantic elevation map. Experiments on public datasets and real excavation scenes demonstrate reliable geometric and semantic perception. In real excavation, the system achieved an average update interval of approximately 1.4 s and a mean elevation error of 12.74cm in dynamically modified regions. Larger errors mainly occur during rapid terrain changes and transient visual disturbances caused by machine motion.
